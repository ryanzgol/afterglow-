OUR PURPOSE

Our purpose:
to be honest.

Our team created Afterglow as a space for people to be open and honest about their hookups, to show that it is normal not to feel the fulfillment society expects from participating in hookup culture.

Here, you can say what you really feel. Our anonymous forum is aimed at taking pressure off and allowing people to speak truthfully about their experiences. We take anonymity seriously, and guarantee that no identifying data is stored anywhere.

Audre Lorde’s concept of the erotic drives our work. Like Lorde, we believe that the erotic is political, and that everyone is entitled to the pursuit of their own erotic wellbeing. Through our forum, we strive to dismantle myths surrounding hookup culture, and remind people that the erotic differs from person to person.

BEFORE YOU JOIN IN

Keep it kind.

Afterglow is a space for people to talk about their own feelings and experiences—not to identify, rate, expose, or make fun of anyone else.

01

Protect privacy. No names, handles, locations, screenshots, or details that could identify another person.

02

Speak from your perspective. Share what you felt, needed, or learned.

03

Leave judgment outside. No slut-shaming, body-shaming, harassment, or mocking someone's choices.

04

Respect boundaries. Consent matters before, during, and after an encounter.
